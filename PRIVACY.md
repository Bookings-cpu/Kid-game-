# Privacy Policy — Rail Rascals!

**Last updated: 14 June 2026**

Rail Rascals! ("the Game", "we", "us") is a single-player endless-runner game
made for children and families. We have built it to be **privacy-first**: the
Game does **not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you
or your child**, and it does not contain analytics, tracking, or behavioural
advertising. This policy explains exactly what the Game does and does not do.

---

## The short version

- **We collect no personal data.** No names, emails, phone numbers, contacts,
  location, photos, microphone, camera, device identifiers, or advertising IDs.
- **Nothing leaves the device.** The Game has no servers of its own and sends
  no data anywhere. It works fully offline.
- **No third-party tracking or analytics.** There are no analytics SDKs,
  trackers, social-media plugins, or behavioural-ad networks.
- **Purchases and ads are gated for grown-ups.** Real-money purchases sit
  behind a parental gate, and any reward-ad feature is opt-in.

---

## What the Game stores (on the device only)

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of **game data in the
browser's local storage on your own device**, under the key
`railrascals_save_v1`. This includes things like:

- your coin balance, best score, and run statistics;
- which characters, pets, and upgrades you have unlocked;
- game settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced-effects);
- daily-reward and mission progress.

This data **never leaves the device**, is **not linked to any identity**, and
contains **no personal information**. You can erase it at any time using the
**Reset** option in the Game's Settings, or by clearing the app/site data in
your device or browser settings.

---

## Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect or process any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email, address, phone, government IDs).
- Precise or coarse location.
- Device or advertising identifiers (IDFA, AAID), IP-based profiles.
- Camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, or health data.
- Browsing history or cross-app/cross-site activity.

The Game requests **no special device permissions**. (It may use the device's
**vibration** function for game feedback; this can be turned off in Settings and
involves no data collection.)

---

## Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Rail Rascals! is intended to be safe for children. Because the Game collects no
personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). We do
not create profiles, serve personalised ads, or use children's data for any
purpose.

If the Game is distributed through a children's or family programme (for
example, Google Play's "Designed for Families" or Apple's Kids Category), it is
configured to meet those programmes' requirements, including the use of only
certified, age-appropriate, non-personalised advertising should advertising be
enabled in a future version.

---

## Purchases and advertising

- **In-app purchases** (optional coin packs) are processed by the platform's own
  billing system (Apple App Store / Google Play). We never see or store your
  payment details. Purchases are placed **behind a parental gate** (a simple
  grown-up check) to help prevent accidental spending by children.
- **Reward ads**, where offered, are **opt-in** — a player chooses to watch a
  short ad in exchange for an in-game reward. When real ads are enabled, they
  will be served by a certified, child-directed-safe ad provider configured for
  **non-personalised** content. No personal data is used to target ads.

---

## Data security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data
for us to lose. Your on-device game progress is protected by your own device's
security.

---

## Changes to this policy

If the Game's data practices ever change (for example, if an online feature is
added in the future), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date
above, and — where required — request appropriate consent before any new data
collection begins.
