Rail Rascals! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

Rail Rascals! (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) is a single-player endless-runner game made for children and families. We built it privacy-first: the Game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child, and contains no analytics, tracking, or behavioural advertising.

The short version

  • We collect no personal data. No names, emails, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, device identifiers, or advertising ID.
  • Nothing leaves the device. The Game has no servers of its own; your progress is saved only on your device, and it works offline.
  • No third-party tracking or analytics.
  • Purchases and ads are gated for grown-ups. Real-money purchases sit behind a parental gate, and any reward ad is opt-in.

What the Game stores (on the device only)

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of game data in your device’s local storage (coin balance, best score, unlocked characters/pets/upgrades, settings, daily-reward and mission progress) under the key railrascals_save_v1. This data never leaves the device, is not linked to any identity, and contains no personal information. You can erase it with the Reset option in Settings, or by clearing the app’s data.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect personal identifiers, location, device or advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID), camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or browsing history. The Game requests no special device permissions. It may use the device’s vibration for feedback (no data involved; switchable in Settings).

Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Rail Rascals! is intended to be safe for children. Because the Game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not create profiles, serve personalised ads, or use children’s data for any purpose. Where the Game is distributed via a families programme (e.g. Google Play “Designed for Families”), it is configured to meet those requirements, including the use of only certified, age-appropriate, non-personalised advertising where advertising is enabled.

Purchases and advertising

In-app purchases (optional coin packs) are processed by the platform’s billing system (Google Play / Apple). We never see or store your payment details. Purchases sit behind a parental gate to help prevent accidental spending by children. Reward ads, where offered, are opt-in — a player chooses to watch a short ad for an in-game reward. When real ads are enabled they are served by a certified, child-directed-safe provider configured for non-personalised content; no personal data is used to target ads.

Data security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to lose. Your on-device progress is protected by your own device’s security.

Changes to this policy

If the Game’s data practices ever change, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above, and request appropriate consent before any new data collection begins.
